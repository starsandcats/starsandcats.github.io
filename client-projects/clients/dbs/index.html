DBS

Upon reading the letters D, B, and S you might be wondering "just what is DBS?" All I can tell you is my best interpretation of what DBS is to me. DBS simply put is a concept, an idea. DBS is a brand, it's a character, it's a man, it's men, it's action and violence, it's chaotic and dancing in the dark, with the one and only MR.DBS being the masked face of it all. You may ask "just who is MR.DBS?" What does he want? Where is he going? What is he doing? What will he do next? Are we in danger? MR.DBS is the mysterious figure who will answer NONE of these questions.
